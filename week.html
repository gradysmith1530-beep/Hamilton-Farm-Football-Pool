Where this comes from. ESPN's public scoreboard, read fresh every time you open the page. Scores and clocks update live during games; the page re-reads itself every minute while anything is in progress.

The line. The spread is shown from your team's side — -3.5 means your team is favored by three and a half. O/U is the total. These are ESPN's posted numbers, not a shopped best price.

Byes. Any team with no game that week shows as off. In college that's an open date, in the NFL it's the bye.
